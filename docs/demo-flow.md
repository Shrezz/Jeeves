# Suggested demo flow

1. Sign in with a synthetic demo account.
2. Upload the fictional text-based homeowners policy and show its ready state.
3. Ask whether the broken window is covered; show the cited policy section and page.
4. Prepare a claim for a $150 repair; explain the $1,000 deductible and $0 estimate.
5. Show a reviewer correction or missing-information question.
6. Review and approve the exact package version, then download the packet labeled “Prepared — not submitted.”
7. Return to the dashboard, confirm a claim status update, and show saved history.
8. Show a Langfuse trace and the evaluation report, including failed cases.

All policyholders, policies, receipts, and incidents in the demo must be fictional.
